Meet the Teacher

About Ruby

Hello! I'm Ruby, the founder of Little English Star School. With a deep love for languages and children, I've dedicated my career to helping young learners discover the joy of English.

After living in New Zealand for seven years and earning my degree in English Literature, I gained a deep understanding of the language and culture. My five years of experience in Montessori schools taught me how children learn best — through play, exploration, and encouragement.

I hold a 120-Hour TESOL/TEFL Certification and an IELTS score of 7.5 (C1 level), ensuring your child receives instruction from a highly qualified teacher.

Our Method

How We Teach

Our teaching approach combines the best of Montessori methods with modern language learning techniques.

Games, role-play, and hands-on activities keep kids engaged and make learning feel like fun, not work.

🗣

Speak from Day 1

We encourage speaking from the very first class. Building confidence to communicate is our top priority.
